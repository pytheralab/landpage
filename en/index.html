    Frequently asked questions

    Does our data leave the company?

    It doesn't have to. For high-security environments, Pythera deploys privately on your own infrastructure. Admins see usage statistics but cannot read employees' conversations.

    How soon will we see results?

    We start with an assessment and a trial on sample data, then pilot in one department and measure results before rolling out company-wide.

    How is pricing structured?

    Delivery fees are based on the time Pythera's team spends with you. AI usage can be paid as a package through Pythera or directly to the provider you choose.

    What if it doesn't deliver?

    Acceptance is based on five conditions agreed up front, including an AI quality threshold. If they aren't met, the work isn't accepted.

    Do our staff need to code?

    No. Staff simply describe the task in plain language, like asking a colleague.

    Start with one small workflow

    In the first assessment, Pythera works with you to pick one workflow, review the data and run a trial on sample data. Only then do we agree scope and acceptance criteria in writing.
