The platform

One platform for knowledge-intensive work.

We're developing a B2B SaaS platform that turns natural-language requests into completed work: reading business information, using external tools and executing multi-step workflows. Here's what is in active development today and what comes next.

In development

Natural-language requests

Describe the outcome in plain language. The platform interprets intent, context and constraints before anything runs.

In development

Document understanding

Extract, compare and summarize information from contracts, invoices, reports and internal knowledge.

In development

Structured outputs

Results return as clean, validated data that drops straight into spreadsheets, CRMs and downstream systems.

Planned

Agents that connect to external tools and APIs to look things up, update records and take action on your behalf.

Planned

Multi-step orchestration

Human-in-the-loop controls

How it works

From request to result, in four steps.

Every workflow follows the same reviewable loop, so results are predictable, traceable and easy to trust.

1

Ask

A team member describes the task in natural language, or a trigger starts it automatically.

intent → plan
2

Understand

The reasoning layer reads the relevant documents and data, and breaks the task into steps.

context → steps
3

Act

Steps execute against connected tools, with checks after each one.

tools → verify
4

Deliver

Structured results are returned for human review, approval or the next system downstream.

output → review
